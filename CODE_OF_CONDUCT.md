# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive collaboration space. Be constructive, assume good intent, and focus discussion on ideas and evidence rather than people.

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discrimination, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove content or limit participation when needed to protect the community and project.

For a conduct concern, contact a maintainer privately through the repository's security-advisory channel. Do not use public issues to report a sensitive incident.
